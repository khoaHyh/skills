# Sound

Sound is half the film. Every effect has a cause on screen, and it comes from the same cue sheet as the picture (`assets/cues.js`), so a timing change moves both.

## Placing a hit

`at(t, file, gain, lead, from, length, {pan, rate, reverse})` schedules one sound for the picture event at `t`.

- **`lead`** is the file's own attack. Subtracting it lands the peak one frame after the picture, because light arriving first reads as natural.
- **`from` / `length`** play a slice: a riser cut to end on the hit, or the first 0.45 s of a long impact.
- **`synth:glass`** is generated by the mixer (a noise snap plus a spray of high partials).

Leads for the bundled HyperFrames SFX, measured with `npx hyperframes audio <file> --json` (0.9.9); re-measure any other file the same way:

| File | Lead (s) | File | Lead (s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| impact-bass-1 | 0.045 | pop | 0.11 |
| impact-bass-2 | 0.015 | click / click-soft | 0.045 |
| notification | 0.221 | ping | 0.32 |
| whoosh / whoosh-short | 0.065 | chime | 0.41 |
| whoosh-cinematic | 2.5 (a swell) | sparkle | 0.02 |
| glitch-1 | 0.095 | riser | peaks at 3.472 |

For a riser into a hit at `t`, start the file at `from = 3.472 − d` and play it for `d − 0.08` seconds, starting at `t − d`.

## Making repeats feel alive (from the Hex stage)

- **Pan by screen x:** `pan = x/1920·1.4 − 0.7` (`panAt` in cues.js).
- **Pitch:** the mixer gives every repeat its own pitch (0.96–1.04). Raise `rate` toward 1.1 as the film escalates.
- **Rotate takes:** alternate two or three files for a repeated action (whoosh / whoosh-short; pop / click / ping).
- **Reverse:** `reverse: true` plays a sound backwards for a rebuild or a rewind.

## The bed

`build-audio.mjs` synthesizes a 120 BPM bed on an Am–F–C–G loop from `music` sections:

| Kind | What plays |
|---|---|
| `room` | A faint pad under the opening |
| `groove` | Kick on the beat, offbeat hats, a clap on 2 and 4 from energy 0.9, sixteenth hats from 1.0, chord stabs above 1.1, bass ducked under the kick |
| `roll` | An accelerating snare roll and a rising sub over the kick |
| `whistle` | A falling slide whistle (for a dive) |
| `pad` | A soft chord for the calm |
| `silence` | Nothing |

A track the person supplies, or one from the HeyGen catalogue (`/media-use`, needs a sign-in), replaces the bed as an `<audio>` clip. Keep the effects in the mix and time the payoff and the end card to the track's strong beats (`npx hyperframes beats`).

## Hits that land

- End every roll and riser 70–80 ms before its downbeat. Without that gap the impact drowns in the build, for the ear and for onset detection alike.
- Drop the music out where the story goes quiet: the dive, the buried beat, the calm, the dread before the button.
- Put each hit 3–6 dB over the bed.

## Levels and proof

The mixer pre-gains the bus (`tanh(x·0.42)`) so loudnorm's second pass stays linear, then normalises to −14 LUFS with a −1.5 dBTP ceiling. The printed `normalization_type` comes from the measuring pass, which always says dynamic. Measure the result instead:

```sh
npx hyperframes audio renders/film.mp4 --json
```

Read `loudness.lufsIntegrated` (−14 ± 1), `loudness.truePeakDb` (≤ −1.5), `clipping.count` (0), and `silence` (only where planned). Then, for each hit, find the nearest onset in `onsets.events` and confirm it is within ±40 ms of the picture time.
